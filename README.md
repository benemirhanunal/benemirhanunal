Hi, I'm Emirhan. I make games, mostly in Unity and sometimes in Unreal, and I've been doing it for about six years: a student club, a classroom, a healthcare VR lab, an online shooter, and finally a game of my own on Steam.

Here's that story as a five-stage run. Each stage loops. Or skip the replays and **[play it yourself in the browser ▶](https://benemirhanunal.github.io/benemirhanunal/)**: walk to the marker at each gate, slash the slimes, reach the festival.

<br>

### Stage 1 · Eskişehir

<img src="assets/stage-1.gif" width="100%" alt="Stage 1: at dawn the hero raises both hands and paper lanterns float up into the sky, a counter climbing to +200 devs">

Studied Computer Engineering at Eskişehir Osmangazi University. There was no place on campus to make games with other people, so I started one. The community grew past 200 developers with jams, workshops and a lot of unfinished prototypes. Some of them got finished.

### Stage 2 · The dojo

<img src="assets/stage-2.gif" width="100%" alt="Stage 2: in front of a dojo the hero cuts a straw post twice while three kneeling students jump up">

Ran a game development training program and taught 100+ students to build their first games. Teaching was the fastest way I found to learn what I didn't actually understand yet.

### Stage 3 · TÜSEB

<img src="assets/stage-3.gif" width="100%" alt="Stage 3: the hero puts on a VR headset and a cyan grid floor, a scan line and hologram panels with a heartbeat open around the hero">

Built clinical VR simulations for medical training on Meta Quest. VR doesn't forgive a dropped frame, so a lot of the job was profiling: batching draw calls, pooling objects, cutting allocations until the headset stayed smooth.

### Stage 4 · Negentra Games

<img src="assets/stage-4.gif" width="100%" alt="Stage 4: at sunset a laggy cyan copy trails behind the hero, two slimes get cut, and the copy snaps into sync">

Worked on an online third-person shooter. Netcode with Photon Fusion, backend on AWS: state sync, lag compensation, and making two players agree on what just happened.

### Stage 5 · TaterUp

<img src="assets/stage-5.gif" width="100%" alt="Stage 5: festival night, fireworks over the lanterns and an achievement toast reading Out on Steam, TaterUp">

My own game, designed, built and shipped. **[TaterUp is on Steam.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3989920/TaterUp/)**

### Next stage

Right now I'm working in Unity 6 across VR, multiplayer and voice-driven AI characters. If you're building something in that space, or just want to talk games, write to me.
